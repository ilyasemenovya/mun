## Шлем космонавта

Режим: встроенный ImageGen, отдельная графика с прозрачностью. Файл: `site/assets/game/helmet.png` (1254 × 1254, RGBA). Оригинальный официант и его четыре кадра сохранены без изменения; шлем накладывается поверх головы при отрисовке. Отверстие визора прозрачно, лицо видно. Положение шлема одинаково во всех позах; поворот при прыжке общий с персонажем. Фон, мебель, физика, управление и рекорд не менялись.

Финальный промпт шлема:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent PNG helmet overlay for a pixel-art game character. Draw ONLY one EMPTY astronaut helmet, strict 2D side profile facing RIGHT, no person, no head, no face, no hair, no body. Compact round cream-white shell over the top and back, small dark circular ear fitting on the LEFT side, slim cream neck collar at bottom. The LARGE OVAL FACE OPENING in the right/front portion must be fully see-through with alpha=0, an actual cutout hole, NOT filled dark blue, white or black. Give the visor a thin dark navy outline and a very small pale-blue highlight only along the outer right rim, leaving all of the central face opening genuinely empty transparent so an existing character face can show through. Retro crisp pixel art with small square steps, cream-white, navy outlines, subtle warm beige shading. Helmet centered, fills 80% of a square canvas, whole helmet visible with generous empty margins. Genuine transparent background with an ALPHA CHANNEL around the helmet and inside the face opening. No checkerboard pattern, no background color, no studio background, no shadow, no ground, no text, no logo, no other objects. This is a transparent overlay asset, not an illustration of a helmet against a background.

## Исходный промпт официанта

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling MUN restaurant runner browser game set on the Moon. Create a precise 2 by 2 grid of FOUR full-body animation frames, genuine alpha transparency, no background, no text, no UI, no separators. The SAME clearly adult male waiter in every cell, strict side view facing RIGHT. White shirt with rolled sleeves, dark navy waistcoat, burnt-orange waist apron, dark trousers and dark shoes, short dark hair. He runs while keeping a round serving tray balanced on his forward bent arm at chest level. On the tray exactly ONE very visible large glass mug of amber BEER with a handle and thick creamy white foam. The mug and tray remain level in every frame. Cheerful focused adult expression. Four consecutive running poses: top-left stride left leg forward, top-right passing phase feet close, bottom-left stride right leg forward, bottom-right second passing phase. Keep torso, tray, mug and head consistent across frames and at the same relative position in each cell; only legs and free arm animate. Feet baselines aligned across cells. Premium clean retro pixel art, crisp blocky pixels, strong silhouette readable at 124px tall, limited cream / midnight navy / terracotta / warm amber palette. Full body and entire tray visible, 15% transparent padding around each character, no cropped parts. Exactly four waiters, four beer mugs total, no astronauts, no helmet, no restaurant scenery, no furniture, no ground shadow. Square 1024x1024 sprite sheet.
